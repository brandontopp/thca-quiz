What Type of THCa Edible Are You Looking At?

Answer two quick questions to find out.

Does the COA list other active cannabinoids (Delta-8 THC, Delta-9 THC, or THCP) alongside THCa?

Does the label or product description mention heat processing or activation during manufacturing?

Type 1

Raw / Pure THCa Product

This product likely contains only unactivated THCa. Don't expect psychoactive effects. If you're looking for an edible that delivers a noticeable experience, you'll want a product with confirmed active THC content instead.

Type 2

Heat-Activated THCa Product

This product's THCa was activated during manufacturing. It should function like a standard THC edible. Verify the COA confirms active THC content and matches the label's claimed potency per serving.

Type 3

THCa + Psychoactive Blend

The THCa in this product is not what's producing the effects. The psychoactive experience comes from the other cannabinoids listed (Delta-8, Delta-9, or THCP). Check the COA to confirm exactly which active compounds are present and at what concentration.
